I am a Staff Research Scientist at Sandia National Laboratories, as a member of the <a href="https://www.sandia.gov/quaac/">Quantum Algorithms and Applications Collaboratory</a> (QuAAC). Previously, I was a <a href="https://www.sandia.gov/quantum/gil-herrera-postdoctoral-fellowship/">Gil Herrera Postdoctoral Fellow</a> at Sandia from 2024–2026. I received my PhD in physics in 2024 from the University of New Mexico, advised by <a href="https://cquic.unm.edu/research/research-groups/miyake-research-group/index.html">Akimasa Miyake</a>. I completed my BSc in physics in 2018 at the University of Maryland, College Park, where I was introduced to quantum computing through the mentorship of <a href="https://shelbykimmel.com/">Shelby Kimmel</a> (now at Middlebury College).
