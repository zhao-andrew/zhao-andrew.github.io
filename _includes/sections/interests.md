Research Interests
==================

Much of my research aims to contribute to an information-theoretic understanding of quantum matter. This is exemplified by the many-body physics of fermions: What is the complexity of simulating different fermionic systems? Where does fermionic quantum information qualitatively diverge from the usual model of qubits? And what does this theory teach us about the computational structure of matter---the electrons and atoms that make up the world around us?[^1]

Alongside such questions, I am more broadly interested in probing the capabilities of quantum computers, primarily through algorithms development and applications to scientific discovery.
